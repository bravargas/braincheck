Safety and Purpose

This local-first tool generates fully fictional check-like images for visual QA, OCR robustness testing, and synthetic dataset generation.

  • No real routing or account numbers are used.
  • Every image includes a strong SAMPLE watermark.
  • Output is intentionally unusable for real financial activity.
